## Design approach

An electrician's customers come in two kinds. One has a dead outlet or a breaker that keeps tripping and wants someone this week. The other is planning a panel upgrade or an EV charger and is collecting estimates.

The homepage splits them early. "Need a repair?" leads to the call button. "Planning a bigger job?" leads to an estimate form that asks for a little more detail, so the owner can quote properly before visiting.

Because people worry about who they let near their wiring, the license number and insurance sit near the top, not buried in the footer.
