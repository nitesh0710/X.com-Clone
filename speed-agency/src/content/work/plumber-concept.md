## Design approach

Someone with water on the kitchen floor isn't reading. They want a number. So the phone number sits in an orange banner at the top of every page, and on a phone the call button is the biggest thing on the first screen.

Below that, the site answers the three questions people ask before they call: do you cover my town, are you licensed, and can you come today. Everything else (services, the about page, reviews) is one tap away for the people who are comparing, not panicking.

The quote form is short on purpose: name, phone, town and a box for what's wrong. Each extra field loses people.
