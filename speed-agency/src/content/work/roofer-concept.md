## Design approach

Roofing is a big purchase. People rarely call the first roofer they find. They look at photos, check reviews and compare two or three companies, often in the days after a storm.

So this site leads with proof of work: a gallery of real jobs, with before and after photos. The main action is a free inspection, which feels like less of a commitment than "get a quote" on a five-figure job.

A storm damage page explains what a homeowner should do next in plain steps. It's useful, and it gives the roofer a page that matches what people search for after bad weather.
